Got Questions?

F.A.Q

Can I do a title search before paying? +

Absolutely — and we encourage it. We'll provide the plot number and details so you can verify at the Ministry of Lands yourself. No commitments until you're satisfied.

Do you allow instalment payments? +

Yes. Pay a 20% deposit to lock in the plot at the current price, then clear the balance in 6–12 months. The title deed is transferred after full payment.

What are the total costs? Any hidden fees? +

Zero hidden fees. The listed price covers everything. Legal fees (stamp duty, transfer costs) are fixed and disclosed upfront before you sign anything.

How long does the title transfer take? +

Once full payment is confirmed, we process the transfer in 3 working days. We have an experienced legal team that moves fast.

Can I build on the plot immediately? +

Yes, all our plots are ready for development. We can also connect you with trusted contractors and architects if needed — just ask.

Do you organise site visits? +

Every Saturday we run group site visits — transport arranged, no charge. Book below or DM us on WhatsApp to sort your date.
